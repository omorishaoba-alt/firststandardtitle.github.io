Retail Users – How You Benefit

FSTL allows individuals to use their assets for verification and financing in a simple, affordable way.

Operating Principle: INPUTS → TRANSFORMATION → OUTPUTS

Process:

  1. Input: Submit your asset details, documents, and identification.
  2. Transformation: FSTL verifies your asset, issues a digital receipt, and optional Title Insurance.
  3. Output: Use the verified asset to access loans, mortgage insurance, or other financial services.

Flat Rate Service: Verification & Collateralisation Fee: ₦50,000 (affordable, value-driven, immediate results)

Institutional Users – How You Benefit

FSTL allows financial institutions and government agencies to convert verified assets into investment-grade capital instruments.

Use Case: Transform verified assets into ABS, MBS, corporate bonds, municipal bonds, or structured financing instruments.

Pricing: 5% of asset value – highly scalable, precise, and transparent.
